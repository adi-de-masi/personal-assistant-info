Privacy policy

personal-assistant is a personal experimentation app operated by Adriano De Masi. Contact: adi.demasi@gmail.com.

Data and purpose

With the owner's consent, the app uses Google email, calendar and file data for requested personal productivity experiments. Data is not sold or used for advertising or to train generalized AI models. Relevant content may be processed by the AI provider selected in Hermes; provider handling and retention depend on that provider and account settings.

Storage and control

Credentials and task records are kept outside this public website with access restrictions. Task records, outputs and backups may remain until removed under their respective retention settings. The owner can request deletion through the contact above and revoke future Google access at Google Account connections. Revocation does not itself delete existing records or backups.

Use and transfer of Google API data will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

This website

These static information pages contain no login form, analytics or third-party scripts. GitHub Pages may process technical request data to serve them; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
